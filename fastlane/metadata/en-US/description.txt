Munnies is the easiest way to track your kids' money without keeping mental notes or paper IOUs.

One simple purchase. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. Pay once and use Munnies for all your family accounts.

Create an account for each child, record allowance, chore money, birthday cash, spending, and savings, and always know the current balance at a glance.

Features:
- Separate accounts for each child
- Fast add and spend actions
- Full transaction history with notes
- Share accounts with your partner or family through iCloud
- Sync across your Apple devices with iCloud
- No Munnies account to create

Munnies is built for parents who want a simple, private way to stay on top of allowances and spending without another recurring subscription.
